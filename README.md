LUMENBREAK — a neon breakout game: bounce the ball with a paddle, shatter
crystal bricks across six hand-designed walls (First Light, Twin Spires,
Ember Heart, Steel Gate, The Maze, The Core), catch falling powerups (wide
paddle, multi-ball, laser, slow ball, extra life), and build a combo
multiplier up to ×5. Ember bricks chain-damage their neighbours; hard bricks
take two hits; steel never breaks. Title screen with a live demo behind it,
HUD (score, best, level, lives, combo, active effect), pause, level-clear
banners, game-over and victory screens, all with restart. Sound is
synthesized WebAudio — no audio files, no network, deterministic seeded RNG,
fixed 1/60 s simulation.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser. Mouse or arrow keys / A D to
move, Space or click to start, launch and fire the laser, P to pause, M to
mute.
